Additive manufacturing · LPBF/DED

Flexycle-AM

Spherical powder built for laser processing.

Highly spherical and free-flowing, Flexycle-AM is calibrated for laser powder bed fusion and directed energy deposition, producing dense, pure-copper parts without the energy cost of virgin atomisation.

Applications: LPBF of pure-copper parts, DED, high-conductivity components
