Additive manufacturing · LPBF/DED

FCP-AM

Spherical powder built for laser processing.

Spheroidal and free-flowing, FCP-AM is tailored to laser powder bed fusion and directed energy deposition. Its rough surface absorbs about twice as much 1070 nm laser light as gas-atomised powder, which widens the process window on standard infrared machines.

Applications: LPBF of pure-copper parts, DED, high-conductivity components

Powder metallurgy · press & sinter

FCP-PM

Rough surface, built for green strength.

FCP-PM's rough particle surfaces interlock under pressure, giving strong green compacts for classic press-and-sinter routes — 90 % of the green strength of water-atomised powder in first trials. The broadest of the six grades, for general powder-metallurgy use where a tight process-specific cut isn't required.

Applications: Press & sinter components, bearings, structural parts
